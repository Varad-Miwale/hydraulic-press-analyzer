Assumptions & Engineering Limitations Registry

Transparent disclosure of model approximations, discrepancies, and unverified data points:

⚠ 9-Ton Holding Load Ambiguity

In all three Hopsan models, the 3 t dead load is simulated as gravity on a 3000 kg mass (-29,430 N) and the press load is applied as a +88,290 N pulse. Net cylinder load is +58,860 N (6 t). If the cylinder itself was required to exert 9 t against gravity, the workpiece resistance must be increased to 117,720 N (12 t).

⚠ Concept 3 Dual-Pump Energy Verification

The exported CSV for Concept 3 contains a single pressure channel. Direct integration across this pressure overstates energy at 125.41 kJ because Pump A dumps to tank at ~0 bar while Pump B supplies ~105 bar. The active cycle value of 33.88 kJ comes from Concept_Comparison.xlsx and requires independent multi-port export verification.

⚠ Relief Valve Setting Discrepancy

All HMF files specify p_max = 1.70e7 Pa (170 bar), whereas the project report states 175 bar. Peak pressures during transition reach ~174.8 bar due to dynamic flow cracking in the valve model.

⚠ Unmodeled Line Losses & Efficiencies

All energy calculations represent raw fluid hydraulic energy at the pump outlet ($P = p \times Q$). The 0.9 pump mechanical efficiency and 10 bar pipe distribution losses specified in Group 2 brief are not added into these curves.
